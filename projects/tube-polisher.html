No. 004 Professional / Industry Work

Tube Polisher Fixture

Process Engineering Co-op at Entegris, January to June 2025

Overview

A fixture that holds nickel tubes exactly 2mm from a grinder-polisher while keeping the operator's hands guarded. It replaced hand filing and took that step from 1 to 2 minutes per tube down to a few seconds.

Time per tube3 to 5 s
Before (hand filing)1 to 2 min
01

The Problem

Operators smoothed the ends of nickel tubes by hand with files. Each tube took 1 to 2 minutes, results varied from person to person, and the constant filing caused hand strain. A grinder-polisher could do the job much faster, but without a fixture it put the operator's hands at risk.

02

Requirements

  • Exact gap. Keep each tube end 2mm off the polishing surface, every time.
  • Guarded hands. The operator's hands stay fully protected from the polisher.
  • No scratches. The nickel tubes are soft, so nothing hard can touch them.
  • Quick loading. Tubes go in and out by hand without tools.
03

Version 1: Failed

The first fixture was machined and used the polisher's base as its reference surface. When it arrived, the 2mm gap could not be held. The base looked flat but had a 2° tilt that had not been caught, which threw the whole fixture out of position.

04

Version 2: New Reference Surface

  • Datum moved. The redesign aligns off the machine's upper surface instead of the base, so the 2mm gap no longer depends on the base at all.
  • Polyurethane inserts. Molded inserts sized for each tube protect the soft nickel from scratches.
  • Tuned fit. The inserts grip the tube firmly but still load easily by hand.
05

Results

Each tube now takes 3 to 5 seconds instead of 1 to 2 minutes, more than 90% faster, with more consistent results and no hand filing.

The lesson I took from version 1: measure the surfaces a part will mate with before releasing it for machining, not after it arrives.
